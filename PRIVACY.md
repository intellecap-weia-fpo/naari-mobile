# Privacy notice

This app is the field client for [naari](https://github.com/intellecap-weia-fpo/naari). The [server's privacy notice](https://github.com/intellecap-weia-fpo/naari/blob/main/PRIVACY.md) covers the data, your rights and how to reach the organisation running the server.

It stores survey answers, and any photos and signatures a survey asks for, on the device until they sync. Signing out clears them. The app asks for location and camera access only for surveys that use them.
